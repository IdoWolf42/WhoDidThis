I've been thinking about the way light falls through my kitchen window in the morning.
There's this specific angle around 7:15 AM in October where the whole counter turns golden.
I keep meaning to take a photo but by the time I have my coffee and remember, it's gone.

My grandmother used to say the best moments are the ones you can't hold onto.
She was right, though I didn't understand it until much later.

I've started keeping a small notebook by the coffee maker. Not for productivity or journaling in
any formal sense — just to write down the things I notice before they slip away.
The way the dog's ears perk up exactly three seconds before anyone rings the doorbell.
The particular creak the third stair makes only in cold weather.

None of this is important. That's the point, I think.
